Why Fake GPS

Why Use a Fake GPS Location?

From protecting your location privacy to testing location-aware Android apps without root, here is why people use a mock location changer and GPS route simulator.

01 · Location Privacy

Mask your location and protect your privacy.

Prevent everyday apps, background trackers, and third-party services from logging your real-time physical coordinates. By masking your live GPS location on Android, you stay in control of when and where your true whereabouts are shared.

02 · Developers & QA Testers

Test geofencing, navigation & location-based apps.

Built for Android developers and QA engineers who need a clean mock location provider without root. Verify geofence entry and exit triggers, localized search results, delivery tracking, and multi-stop GPS route simulations with realistic corner slowdown, signal drift, and custom speeds—all from your desk.

03 · Social & Fun

Pranking friends with a live pin anywhere on Earth.

Surprise friends and family by dropping a fake GPS pin in Tokyo, Paris, or a remote island in one tap. Bookmark your favourite prank locations or set a scheduled auto-stop timer so your phone automatically switches back when you’re done.

04 · Global Discovery

Access geo-restricted content & local services.

Explore region-specific app features, local directories, events, and location-based content before travelling to a new city or country—all by switching your device’s reported GPS location in seconds.
